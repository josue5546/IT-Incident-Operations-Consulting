# Management Presentation

## Overview

This folder contains the seven-slide executive presentation developed to communicate the main findings and recommendations from the IT service management analysis.

The presentation condenses the wider analytical work into a focused management discussion, highlighting the operational issues, supporting evidence and decisions required to move towards improvement.

## Presentation Coverage

- **Executive assessment:** The main operational challenges and the recommended direction.
- **Incident performance:** Key findings relating to incident volumes, resolution times and data completeness.
- **Process efficiency:** Evidence linking repeated handling and reassignment activity with longer resolution times.
- **Routing opportunities:** Frequently observed support-team transfers and routing returns.
- **Management recommendations:** Prioritised operational actions, responsible roles and safeguards.
- **Implementation roadmap:** A proposed 12-week pilot with performance measures and evaluation criteria.
- **Conclusion:** The case for improving processes and validating operational requirements before introducing wider automation.

## Purpose

The presentation is designed to communicate the findings to managers and other stakeholders without requiring them to review the full technical analysis.

It focuses on three questions: what the evidence shows, why the findings matter and what management should consider doing next.

The recommendations emphasise clearer incident ownership, targeted escalation reviews, stronger data-quality controls and carefully tested process improvements.

The presentation is supported by the detailed consulting report, Python analysis and Power BI dashboards. It presents a proposed course of action rather than claiming that operational changes have already been delivered.
