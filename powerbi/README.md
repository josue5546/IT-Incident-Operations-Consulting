# Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This folder contains the three-page Power BI dashboard developed to present incident-management performance and highlight opportunities for operational improvement.

The dashboards bring together the validated analytical findings in a clear, accessible format designed to support management discussions and decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides an overall assessment of incident-management performance, including incident volumes, median and 90th-percentile resolution times, reassignment rates, reopened incidents and handling activity.

It also identifies three management priorities: repeated handling, prolonged resolution times and incomplete incident records.

### 2. Resolution & Process Efficiency

Examines how incident resolution times vary with reassignment frequency and handling activity.

The dashboard highlights groups of incidents associated with longer resolution times and proposes targeted reviews to investigate potential process bottlenecks.

### 3. Routing & Transformation Opportunities

Analyses support-team transfers and incidents that return to previously assigned teams.

It identifies frequently occurring routing patterns and highlights where clearer ownership, assignment guidance or process improvements may be worth investigating.

## Purpose

The dashboards translate technical analysis into information that managers can understand and act upon.

Rather than displaying performance measures alone, each page connects the findings to operational questions and proposed management actions.

All recommendations are based on historical observations. No process changes or performance improvements are presented as having already been implemented.
