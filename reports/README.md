# Consulting Report

## Overview

This folder contains the management consulting report developed from the SQL analysis, Python statistical findings and Power BI dashboards.

The report examines incident-management performance, identifies areas of operational friction and proposes practical steps to improve resolution efficiency, support-team coordination and data reliability.

It is structured around a central management question: which operational issues should be prioritised, and what evidence is needed before implementing changes?

## Report Coverage

- **Executive assessment:** A summary of the main operational challenges and recommended management priorities.
- **Performance analysis:** Findings relating to resolution times, reassignment activity, incident handling and data completeness.
- **Statistical evidence:** Interpretation of the Python analysis, including important analytical limitations.
- **Routing and process improvement:** Assessment of repeated support-team transfers and potential ownership issues.
- **Practical recommendations:** Prioritised actions covering escalation reviews, clearer assignment rules, data-quality controls and targeted automation.
- **Implementation roadmap:** A proposed 12-week pilot to test improvements before considering wider implementation.
- **Client questions and information gaps:** Areas requiring clarification with management before final decisions can be made.

## Purpose

The report demonstrates how data analysis can support management consulting decisions.

Its recommendations focus on practical changes that could be investigated and tested, rather than assuming that every repeated transfer or long-running incident represents an avoidable inefficiency.

The proposed actions are supported by analytical evidence, but they have not been implemented or evaluated in a live operational environment.

The overall objective is to provide management with a structured basis for deciding where to investigate, what to improve and how to measure whether proposed changes are successful.
